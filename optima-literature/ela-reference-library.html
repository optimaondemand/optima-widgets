ELA Reference Library

Every title on the Optima book list, K–12, with what it costs a family and what a teacher can hand out for free. Tick the box on any card to build a purchase list.

What the words mean

★ TaughtOn the OAO 2026-27 approved book list. Every title here carries it. Where a course has an audited edition, the gold note on the card names it, and that edition is what settles rights
CLTBy an author in the Classic Learning Test Author Bank. CLT publishes authors, not titles, so this marks the author, not the exact book
FreePublic domain. The free text is the same text.
SimilarFree version available in a different translation or edition.
BuyIn copyright. Students need a licensed copy.
ArchaicTranslation published before 1850 - expect dated, difficult English
OlderTranslation from 1850-1899 - readable, but not modern English
VerifyA date or rights claim that still needs a human check

A free PDF on a school or personal website is not a licence. Free versions here come only from Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Wikisource, the Library of Congress, the Folger, and government archives. Where a work is public domain but no trusted source has been found, the card says No source rather than guessing.

Titles marked Similar are a different translation or edition from the one the book list specifies. They are fine for reference and for a student who cannot buy the book, but do not assign by page or line number across two different versions.
