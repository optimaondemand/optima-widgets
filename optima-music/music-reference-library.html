How this catalogue was built

Every YouTube video used across the K-12 Optima music courses, with what it is, which course and module uses it, and where it meets the art and ELA libraries.

Which course teaches a video

MI-1, MI-2, MI-3 and one M/J Music Theory 1 section export an IDENTICAL 116-video pool, so a Canvas export cannot say which course teaches a video. Course attribution therefore comes only from the renovated build folders. A chip on a card is therefore evidence from a rebuilt module, not from an export. Where a video is that course's own live Canvas content and the course has no rebuilt folder yet, the chip is outlined rather than solid.

What a topic tag claims

scope="video" means the video itself is that thing. scope="lesson" means the Optima lesson using it is about that thing. Every tag names the string that justified it. Hover any tag to read the exact string that justified it. A dashed outline marks a tag that describes the lesson rather than the video.

What is not stored here

Titles, channels, thumbnails and links only. No lyrics and no transcripts are held anywhere in this catalogue or its build kit — Optima links out to music rather than reproducing it. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play, and the player is the youtube-nocookie embed, so browsing this shelf does not build a watch history.
